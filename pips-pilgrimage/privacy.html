Pip's Pilgrimage

Privacy Policy

Effective 20 October 2026 · Updated 22 October 2026

This policy explains what information Pip's Pilgrimage (the "game"), made by Zoluwu ("we", "us"), uses, why, and the choices you have. If you have any questions, email inform.zolesto@gmail.com.

In short

  • We don't ask for your name, email, location, contacts or photos, and the game has no accounts or sign-in.
  • Your progress (distance, coins, gems, upgrades, settings) is saved only on your phone.
  • The game shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect some device information to show and measure those ads, as explained below.
  • Purchases are handled by Google Play. We never see your card or payment details.

Information saved on your device

The game saves your progress and settings in a file on your phone so you can carry on where you left off. This file is not sent to us or to anyone else. Uninstalling the game or clearing its storage deletes it. Because we don't run servers or keep copies, we can't restore lost progress.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads using Google AdMob: optional video ads you choose to watch (for example, to revive or double your coins) and occasional full-screen ads between runs. To show, limit and measure these ads and to prevent fraud, Google's software in the game may collect:

  • Device identifiers, such as your phone's advertising ID and app set ID
  • Your IP address, which can indicate your approximate location
  • Basic device information, such as model, operating system and language
  • How you interact with ads (for example, views and taps) and diagnostic information about the ads

This information goes to Google, not to us. Google may use it as described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and in the Google Privacy Policy.

Ads in the game are limited to content suitable for teens.

Purchases (Google Play)

You can buy gems or Remove Ads through Google Play's billing system. Google processes the payment under the Google Privacy Policy. The game only receives confirmation of what was bought, so it can give you the item and restore Remove Ads if you reinstall.

Your choices

  • Ad consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland): the game asks for your choice before showing personalised ads, with options to consent, not consent or manage your choices. You can change it at any time under About → Privacy settings in the game.
  • US state privacy rights: if you live in California or another US state with a privacy law, you can opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of your information for personalised ads under About → Privacy settings → Do not sell or share my personal information. We keep no personal information ourselves; if you want to ask about the data Google's ad services hold, contact us and we'll help.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete your phone's advertising ID in Android's settings (usually Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Settings → Google → Ads).
  • Fewer ads: the Remove Ads purchase turns off the ads between runs. Optional reward ads stay available, but only play if you choose to watch them.
  • Deleting your data: uninstall the game or clear its storage. We hold no other copy.

Permissions

The game uses internet and network-state access only to load ads and to talk to Google Play for purchases. It doesn't use your camera, microphone, contacts, files or location permission.

Children

The game is made for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child under 13 has used the game in a way that shared their information, contact us and we'll help.

Security

We don't run servers or store your information ourselves. Information collected by Google for ads and purchases is handled under Google's own security practices.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
